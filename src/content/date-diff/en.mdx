## What does it do?

Two calculators in one:

- **Between two dates**: the difference broken down into years, months, days, hours and minutes, plus the totals in days, weeks, hours and minutes and the number of **business days**.
- **Add or subtract**: the date you get by adding or subtracting years, months, weeks, days, hours or minutes from a given date.

## Examples

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Days between 1 January and 24 December 2026 | 357 days |
| 30 days after 15 March | 14 April |
| 1 month after 31 January 2024 | 29 February 2024 |
| Business days from Monday to the following Monday | 5 |

## In code

```js
import { differenceInDays, differenceInBusinessDays, addMonths } from "date-fns";

differenceInDays(new Date("2026-12-24"), new Date("2026-01-01")); // 357
addMonths(new Date("2024-01-31"), 1); // 2024-02-29
```
