## What does it do?

It **creates CSS gradients visually**: choose **linear, radial or conic**, add as many **color stops** as you want, move them and set the angle or center while the preview updates. Paste an existing gradient to keep editing it.

## Example

`linear-gradient(135deg, #0ea5e9 0%, #6366f1 45%, #d946ef 100%)` goes diagonally from sky blue through indigo to fuchsia.

## Tips

- Use the gradient as `background` (or `background-image`), not as `background-color`.
- Stops at the same position create a hard edge: `red 50%, blue 50%` draws two halves.
- A conic gradient with hard edges makes a pie chart in pure CSS.
