## What does it do?

It generates a complete **fluid type scale**: from small text to the largest heading, every size is a `clamp()` value that grows smoothly between a small and a large screen. You choose the **base size** and the **ratio** for each end.

## Export

- **CSS variables**: `--step-0`, `--step-1`… ready for `font-size: var(--step-3)`.
- **Tailwind v4**: an `@theme` block that redefines `text-base`, `text-lg`, `text-2xl`…
- **Tailwind v3**: the `fontSize` object for `tailwind.config.js`.

## Common ratios

| Ratio | Name | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.125 | Major second | Dense, interfaces |
| 1.2 | Minor third | Balanced, blogs |
| 1.25 | Major third | Clear hierarchy |
| 1.333 | Perfect fourth | Editorial |
| 1.5 | Perfect fifth | Landing pages |
