## What does it do?

It is a **library of ready-made regular expressions** for the validations every project needs: email, URL, Spanish DNI and NIE, IBAN, international and Spanish phone numbers, postal codes, strong passwords, dates, times, hex colours, IPv4 addresses, slugs, UUIDs and usernames.

Each pattern comes with:

- A **description** of what it accepts and its limits.
- **Examples** that should match and that should not, already tested.
- An **explanation** of every part of the regex.
- **Copy for** JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML, Zod, Angular and Python.
- Buttons to open it in the **Tester** or the **Builder** to adapt it.

## Tip

Validate in the browser for a quick response to the user, and **always validate again on the server**: a client-side regex can be bypassed.
