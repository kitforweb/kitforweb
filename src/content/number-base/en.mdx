## What does it do?

It has two modes:

- **Numbers:** type a value in decimal, hexadecimal, binary or octal and see it in all the others at once. Negative numbers and numbers of any size are supported.
- **Text ↔ bytes:** turns text into its UTF-8 bytes written in hex, binary or decimal, and back. Handy for protocols, network dumps or CTF challenges.

## Quick reference

| Decimal | Hex | Binary | Octal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | `0xA` | `0b1010` | `0o12` |
| 255 | `0xFF` | `0b1111 1111` | `0o377` |
| 4096 | `0x1000` | `0b1 0000 0000 0000` | `0o10000` |

## Text example

`Hola` in UTF-8 is `48 6f 6c 61` in hex, or `01001000 01101111 01101100 01100001` in binary.
