## What does it do?

It **compresses text** with gzip, deflate (zlib) or raw deflate and gives you the result in **Base64**, and it **decompresses** Base64 payloads back to text. Typical uses:

- Reading compressed **API payloads**, **cookies** or **JWT claims** that someone gzipped to save space.
- Inspecting values stored compressed in **databases**, **logs** or **message queues**.
- Checking **how much** a JSON response would shrink with compression.

## How to use it

1. Paste text to compress it; the sizes before and after and the savings appear above the result.
2. Press **Swap** to decompress Base64. Leave the format on **Auto-detect** unless you know it.
3. Data URLs and Base64URL are accepted too.

Small inputs may grow after compression because of the format's headers: compression pays off from a few hundred bytes of repetitive data.
