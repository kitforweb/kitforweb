## What does it do?

It converts images to **WebP**, **AVIF** (if your browser can encode it), **JPEG** or **PNG**, with **adjustable quality** and an optional **maximum width**, and shows the new size next to the original so you can see how much you save.

## Why it matters

Images are usually the heaviest part of a page. Serving WebP or AVIF instead of JPEG and PNG often halves their weight, which improves Largest Contentful Paint and saves mobile data.

## Tips

- Resize before compressing: a 4000px photo shown at 800px wastes most of its bytes.
- Keep PNG for images that need exact pixels, such as diagrams with fine lines.
- Transparent images become a white background when converted to JPEG.
