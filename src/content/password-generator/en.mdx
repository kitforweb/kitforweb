## What does it do?

It generates **secure random passwords** and **secrets** directly in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent, not even to analytics.

- Choose the **length** (4 to 128) and the character sets: lower case, upper case, digits and symbols.
- **Exclude ambiguous characters** such as `0`, `O`, `1`, `l` and `I` for passwords someone has to read or type.
- See the **entropy** in bits and a strength rating.
- Get a ready-to-paste `.env` line with a random secret in hex, Base64 or Base64URL.

## Entropy

Entropy measures how many guesses an attacker needs: `length × log2(pool size)`.

| Password | Pool | Entropy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 lower-case letters | 26 | 38 bits — weak |
| 12 mixed characters | 88 | 77 bits — fair |
| 20 mixed characters | 88 | 129 bits — very strong |
