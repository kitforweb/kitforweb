## What does it do?

It converts **HTML into valid JSX** for React, Preact or Solid, handling what usually breaks when you paste HTML into a component:

- `class` → `className`, `for` → `htmlFor` and the other renamed attributes.
- **Inline styles** become objects: `style="margin-top: 4px"` → `style={{ marginTop: "4px" }}`.
- **Void elements** are self-closed: `<br>` → `<br />`, `<img>` → `<img />`.
- **SVG attributes** in camelCase: `stroke-width`, `fill-rule`, `xlink:href`.
- **Comments** become `{/* … */}` and **braces in text** are escaped.
- Several root elements are wrapped in a **fragment**, and optionally everything goes inside a **component**.

## Example

`<label for="email" class="label">Email</label>` becomes `<label htmlFor="email" className="label">Email</label>`.
