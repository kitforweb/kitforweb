## What does it do?

It lets you **build a regular expression without writing it**. Drag blocks written in plain language onto the canvas — *starts with*, *digit*, *one of*, *either*, *repeat 2 to 4 times* — and the regex is written for you as you go, with an explanation of every part.

## How to use it

1. **Drag** blocks from the palette to the canvas, or **tap** them to append them.
2. Put blocks inside a **Group** or an **Either** block to nest them.
3. **Select** a block to change its text, its characters or how many times it repeats.
4. Test it right away in **Quick test**, one case per line.
5. Copy it as a literal or **export** it for JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML `pattern`, Zod or Angular.

Undo and redo with the buttons or with **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Shift+Z**.

## Example

| Blocks | Regex |
| --- | --- |
| Starts with · Digit ×3 · Exact text “-” · Digit ×4 · Ends here | `^\d{3}-\d{4}$` |
| Starts with · Either (“http” / “https”) · Exact text “://” | `^(?:http|https):\/\/` |

The **Share** button stores the pattern in the link (after the `#`), so you can send it to a colleague.
