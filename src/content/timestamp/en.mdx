## What does it do?

It converts between **Unix time** (epoch) and human dates:

- **Epoch → date**: seconds, milliseconds, microseconds or nanoseconds, detected by their length.
- **Date → epoch**: ISO 8601, RFC 2822 or a date picked in the calendar.
- Every representation at once: ISO 8601, UTC, your local time with its time zone, a relative time ("3 hours ago"), weekday, ISO week and day of the year.

The current timestamp is shown live at the top, ready to copy.

## Getting the current timestamp

| Language | Seconds | Milliseconds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JavaScript | `Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000)` | `Date.now()` |
| Python | `int(time.time())` | `int(time.time() * 1000)` |
| PHP | `time()` | `(int) (microtime(true) * 1000)` |
| Bash | `date +%s` | `date +%s%3N` |
| SQL (PostgreSQL) | `extract(epoch from now())` | |
