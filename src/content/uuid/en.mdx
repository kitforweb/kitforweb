## What does it do?

It **generates UUIDs** (universally unique identifiers) in version 4 (random) or version 7 (time-ordered), one at a time or up to 1,000 at once, and **inspects** any UUID you paste: it validates it and tells you its version, its variant and, for time-based versions (1, 6 and 7), when it was created.

## How to use it

1. Choose **v4** or **v7** and how many you need. Toggle upper case, hyphens or braces to match your codebase.
2. Copy the list, or press **Regenerate** for a new batch.
3. Paste any UUID on the left to inspect it.

## Anatomy of a UUID v7

```
0190a5b8-6e1f-7c3a-9b2d-4e5f6a7b8c9d
└──────┬─────┘ │ └┬┘ │
 Unix ms (48 bits) │  │  └ variant (10xx)
             version 7  random
```
