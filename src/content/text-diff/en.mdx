## What does it do?

It **compares two texts** (code, configuration, contracts, SQL…) and shows what changed **line by line and word by word**, either **side by side** or **inline** like a pull request.

## Options

- **Ignore whitespace**: indentation or trailing spaces do not count as changes.
- **Ignore case**: `SELECT` and `select` are the same.
- **Only changes**: hides long unchanged regions and keeps three lines of context.
- **Swap**: exchanges the original and the modified text.

## Typical uses

- Check what a formatter or a refactor really changed.
- Compare two versions of a config file between environments.
- Review a text before and after an edit when there is no version control.
