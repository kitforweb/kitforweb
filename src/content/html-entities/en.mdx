## What does it do?

It **converts special characters into HTML entities** so they display as text instead of being interpreted as markup, and **decodes** entities back into characters.

| Character | Named | Decimal | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `&` | `&amp;` | `&#38;` | `&#x26;` |
| `<` | `&lt;` | `&#60;` | `&#x3C;` |
| `"` | `&quot;` | `&#34;` | `&#x22;` |
| `é` | `&eacute;` | `&#233;` | `&#xE9;` |

## How to use it

1. Paste text or HTML to encode it. Choose named, decimal or hexadecimal references.
2. Enable **Encode all non-ASCII** to turn accents and emoji into references too.
3. Press **Swap** to decode entities found in a page, a database or an API response.

The decoder understands the full list of HTML5 named references, such as `&nbsp;`, `&mdash;` or `&hellip;`.
