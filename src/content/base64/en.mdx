## What does it do?

It **encodes** text or files to Base64 and **decodes** Base64 back to text, images or files. It supports:

- **UTF-8** text, including accents and emoji.
- **Base64URL** (`-` and `_`, no padding), as used by JWTs.
- **Data URLs** (`data:image/png;base64,…`) in both directions.
- **Files** of any type, with a preview for images and a download button when decoding binary data.

## How to use it

1. To encode, type text or press **Encode a file**.
2. To decode, switch to **Decode** and paste Base64, Base64URL or a data URL. Whitespace and line breaks are ignored.
3. Press **Swap** to send the result back to the input in the other mode.

## Size

Base64 turns every 3 bytes into 4 characters, so the encoded text is about **33% larger** than the original. Embedding big images as data URLs makes pages heavier and prevents caching; keep it for small icons.
