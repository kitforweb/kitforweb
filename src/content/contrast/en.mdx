## What does it do?

It checks the **contrast ratio** between a text color and its background following **WCAG 2.2**, and tells you which levels pass: **AA and AAA** for normal and large text, and **3:1 for UI components** such as icons and input borders.

When a level fails, it suggests the **closest color that passes**, with a button to apply it, so you fix accessibility without redesigning.

## Minimum ratios

| Content | AA | AAA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal text | 4.5:1 | 7:1 |
| Large text | 3:1 | 4.5:1 |
| Icons, borders of controls | 3:1 | — |
