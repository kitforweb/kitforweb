## What does it do?

It converts an **array of JSON objects to CSV** — ready to open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers — and **CSV back to JSON**. Rows with different keys are supported: the columns are the union of every key found.

## How to use it

1. Paste a JSON array on the left to get the CSV on the right.
2. Pick the **delimiter**: comma, semicolon, tab or pipe.
3. Press **Swap** to convert CSV to JSON. In this direction **Auto** detects the delimiter for you.

## Example

```json
[{ "id": 1, "address": { "city": "London" }, "tags": ["a", "b"] }]
```

```csv
id,address.city,tags.0,tags.1
1,London,a,b
```

Values containing the delimiter, quotes or line breaks are quoted automatically, following RFC 4180.
