## What does it do?

It decodes **X.509 certificates** in PEM format — the ones used by HTTPS, mTLS and code signing — and shows:

- **Subject** and **issuer**, and whether the certificate is self-signed or a CA.
- The **domains** it covers (Subject Alternative Names) and IP addresses.
- **Validity** dates with a warning when it has expired or expires in less than 30 days.
- **Public key** and **signature** algorithms, key size and key usage.
- **SHA-256** and **SHA-1** fingerprints, to pin or compare certificates.

Paste a whole **chain** (leaf, intermediates, root) and each certificate is shown in order.

## Command-line equivalent

```bash
openssl x509 -in cert.pem -noout -text
openssl x509 -in cert.pem -noout -fingerprint -sha256
```
