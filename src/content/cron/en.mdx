## What does it do?

It **explains a cron expression in plain words** — for example `*/15 9-17 * * 1-5` becomes "Every 15 minutes, between 09:00 and 17:59, Monday through Friday" — and lists the **next 10 runs** in your local time or UTC. You can also **build** an expression field by field or start from a preset.

## Cron syntax

```
┌───────── minute (0–59)
│ ┌─────── hour (0–23)
│ │ ┌───── day of month (1–31)
│ │ │ ┌─── month (1–12 or JAN–DEC)
│ │ │ │ ┌─ day of week (0–6 or SUN–SAT, 0 = Sunday)
│ │ │ │ │
* * * * *
```

| Expression | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `0 * * * *` | every hour, on the hour |
| `0 9 * * 1-5` | weekdays at 09:00 |
| `30 2 1 * *` | the 1st of each month at 02:30 |
| `0 0 * * 0` | every Sunday at midnight |
| `@daily` | every day at 00:00 |

A sixth field at the beginning, for **seconds**, is also supported, as used by Spring, Quartz and node-cron.
