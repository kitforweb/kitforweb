## What does it do?

It creates **QR codes** for:

- **Text or URLs**: links to your site, a menu, a form.
- **WiFi**: guests join your network by scanning, without typing the password.
- **Contacts** (vCard): name, phone, email, company and website, saved in one scan.

Choose the **colors**, the **error correction** level, add a **logo** in the center and download it as **PNG** or **SVG** (vector, perfect for print).

## Tips

- Keep a strong contrast: dark modules on a light background. Inverted colors fail in some readers.
- Leave the quiet zone (the white margin): without it, scanning is less reliable.
- Shorter content means a smaller, easier-to-scan code.
