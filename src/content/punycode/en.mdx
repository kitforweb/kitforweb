## What does it do?

It converts **internationalised domain names** — with accents, `ñ`, umlauts or non-Latin scripts — into their **ASCII Punycode** form (`xn--…`) and back.

| Unicode | Punycode |
| --- | --- |
| `españa.com` | `xn--espaa-rta.com` |
| `münchen.de` | `xn--mnchen-3ya.de` |
| `例え.テスト` | `xn--r8jz45g.xn--zckzah` |

## How to use it

1. Paste one domain, email address or URL per line.
2. Only the host is converted: paths, query strings and the local part of emails stay as they are.
3. Press **Swap** to decode `xn--` domains back to Unicode.

You need the Punycode form when configuring DNS records, TLS certificates, CORS allow-lists or cookies for an internationalised domain.
