## What does it do?

It shows **one moment in several time zones at the same time**. Choose a date and time in any zone and you instantly see the local time in every city on your list, with its UTC offset and a marker when it falls within working hours (9:00 to 18:00).

## How to use it

1. Pick the **date and time** and the **time zone** it refers to.
2. **Add cities or zones** with the search box: "Tokyo", "Sao Paulo", "America/Denver"…
3. Rows outside working hours are dimmed, and a **+1d** or **−1d** marks a different calendar day.
4. Copy the list to paste it in an invitation or a chat.

Your list of zones is remembered in this browser for next time.

## Useful for

- Finding a meeting slot that works for a distributed team.
- Checking the server time in logs and cron jobs that run in UTC.
- Debugging bugs that only happen "after midnight" in another country.
