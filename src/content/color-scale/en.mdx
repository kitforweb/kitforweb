## What does it do?

From **one base color** it generates the **eleven shades 50–950** that Tailwind uses, ready for backgrounds, borders, buttons and text. The scale is computed in **OKLCH**, keeping your hue and lowering the chroma at the extremes, so pale and dark shades do not look washed out or neon.

## Export

- **Tailwind v4**: `--color-brand-500` variables inside `@theme`, so `bg-brand-500` just works.
- **Tailwind v3**: an object for `theme.extend.colors`.
- **CSS variables** and **SCSS** variables, in OKLCH or HEX.
